How it works

  1. Send us four exports

    AR aging, job list, change order log, and the pay apps on closed jobs. From Sage, Foundation, Vista, QuickBooks, or a spreadsheet. We sign an NDA first.

  2. We sweep 24 months of closed jobs

    Every retention line checked against the release deadline. Every approved change order checked against what was billed. Every open request aged. Every lien window dated.

  3. You get the report and the paperwork

    A ledger of what's owed, ranked by how likely it is to come in, and a drafted request for each line. Your office reviews and sends. Fourteen days from the day we get the exports.

What we never do

  • Contact your GC, an owner, or a surety. Every request goes out under your name, from your office.
  • Take a percentage of what comes in. The fee is flat and you know it before we start.
  • File liens, stop notices, or demand letters. We draft release requests and billing packages. Anything adversarial goes to your attorney.
  • Keep your data. We work from your exports and delete them when the audit closes.

Pricing

One flat fee, set by your annual revenue. You hear the number in the first five minutes of the first call. No percentage of what comes in and no hourly billing.

After the audit, we can keep running closeout and deadline tracking on every new job, month to month, if you want it.
